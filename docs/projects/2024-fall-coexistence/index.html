Human-Wildlife Coexistence: Small Scale Farmer Perspectives on Techniques for Mitigating Predation in Southern Chile

Research on small-scale farmers in the Araucania Region of Chile, detailing perspectives and techniques used for coexistence with predators and wildlife
Social-Ecological Sustainability
Ethnographic Research

Part of the UCEAP Social-Ecological Sustainability Program involved a group project with a group of 7 students. The group conducted research via interviews, maps and farm tours, and card sorts to receive insight into how local farmers co-existed with wildlife? What techniques did they use to prevent their livestock from being killed by predators? What predators did they see the most? Where? What were the most efficient alternatives to killing predators and wildlife?

Part of the results detailed which predators had killed livestock on the farmers properties, i.e. predation events, and which predators had simply been spotted on the farmers’ properties and thus posed risk of predation, i.e. sightings. The bar graphs below visualize which predators had attacked or had been seen on farmers properties.

Figure 1: Predation Events

#making bar graph for predation graph

#base layer: ggplot
ggplot(data = predation_clean,
       mapping = aes(x = predator,
                     y = farmers_who_reported_attacks)) +
  #adding geometry: bar graph
  #customizing color to match website as darkbrown
  geom_bar(stat = "identity", fill = darkbrown) +
  # changing theme from default
  theme_classic() +
  # Changing axes labels
  labs(x = "Predator",
       y = "Farmers Who Reported Predation",
       # wrapping title
       title = str_wrap("Predation events in Small-scale Farms in the Araucania Region of Chile",
                        #controlling where line break happens
                        width = 50)) +
  #customizing colors to match website
  theme(plot.background = element_rect(fill = khaki3),
        panel.background = element_rect(fill = khaki),
        axis.title = element_text(family = "serif", # axis title serif
                                  color = darkbrown), # axis title darkbrown
        axis.text = element_text(family = "serif", # axis font serif
                                  color = darkbrown), # axis text color brown
        axis.line = element_line(color = darkbrown), # axis line brown
        plot.title = element_text(hjust = 0.5, # center title
                                  family = "serif", # serif font
                                  color = darkbrown, # changing color
                                  size = 20), # bigger title
        legend.position = "none") # getting rid of legend

Figure 1: Predation Events were frequently caused by stray dogs, peucos (hawks), and vison (introduced mink). Data collected in the Araucania Region of Chile as part of UCEAP program (Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, 2024). Bars represent amount of farms that had reported attacks by a specific predator on the property. Stray dogs (domestic dog), Peucos (hawks), and Vison (mink) were the most commonly reported predators that attacked livestock followed by Güiña (wild cat), fox, and puma. Each category represents a different predator.
